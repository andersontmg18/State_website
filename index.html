About the State

Illinois, admitted as the 21st state on December 3, 1818, is a major Midwestern state known as the Land of Lincoln and the Prairie State. With its capital in Springfield and largest city Chicago, it is the sixth most populous U.S. state, featuring a diverse landscape, significant agricultural industry, and rich history.
